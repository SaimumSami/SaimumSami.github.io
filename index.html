About Myself

I am an undergraduate in Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE) at the American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB), with a strong foundation in solar PV design, circuit simulation and engineering software.

My interests are renewable energy, power conversion and electromagnetic modelling. I enjoy taking an idea, such as a hybrid solar-tidal microgrid for rural coastal communities, and testing it through simulation in MATLAB/Simulink and COMSOL Multiphysics.

Career goal: to secure an entry-level engineering position where I can apply my technical skills, contribute to renewable energy and electrical engineering solutions, and keep growing in a professional environment.
